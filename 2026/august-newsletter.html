From the Commissioner

The 5th tournament of the year was played at Heatherwoode on a wet day.

But before giving the results I must inform those who haven’t heard that one of our Middletown Area Golf Association golfers, Dick Freeman, suddenly passed away on the day after our Kings Island Tourney. Dick played in 153 tournaments over a 31 year span in MAGA. As we keep getting older we must get checked out to prevent anymore sudden medical surprises.

Our next tournaments will be September 19th and 20th will be at Indian Ridge and Shaker Run.

Up Next: September 19 — Indian Ridge

On Saturday September 19th we will play Indian Ridge for the first time. Tee times will start at 10:06 and entry fee will be $69 plus side games. The format for this tournament is a two man best ball. Pairings will be determined by blind draw.

September 20 — Shaker Run Makeup

Also I have added Shaker Run on Sunday September 20th starting around 8:30. This will make up for the rainout earlier in the year. The format for this tourney is you can pick your foursome but we will blind draw for three man teams after golf. Format is 1 score on par 5’s, 2 balls on par 4’s and all 3 balls on par 3’s. Entry fee is $79 plus side games.

The last tournament will be at Wildwood on October 17th around 12:00.

Horn Scramble — October 20

Anyone that is interested on October 20th the Horn Scramble will be played. The format is a 3 man scramble. Sixsomes will be played at Wildwood. You must use at least 3 tee shots from each golfer. Entry fee is somewhere around $100 and they blind draw for teams by handicap. Usually they payout around 8 places. Tee times start around 10:00 and are a shotgun start.
