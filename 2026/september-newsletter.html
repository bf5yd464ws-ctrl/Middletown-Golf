Volume 26, Issue 6 September 2026 Indian Ridge & Shaker Run

From the Commissioner

The 6th & 7th tournament of the year was played at Indian Ridge and Shaker Run. Our next tournament will be October 17th and will be at Wildwood starting around 12:00.

The Saturday tourney was played at Indian Ridge for the first time. The course was in good shape and some people complained because of the length. If anyone happened to find a Titleist 9 iron and put it in their bag contact Russ because I left it near the putting green.

Sundays tourney was played at Shaker Run with a small field of 11 golfers. It was extremely wet and soggy all over.
